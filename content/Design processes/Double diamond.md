The Double Diamond design process is a framework used to solve problems through four stages:

- **Discover** – Explore and understand the problem by gathering insights and user needs.
- **Define** – Identify the key problem to focus on.
- **Develop** – Generate and test different ideas and solutions.
- **Deliver** – Select, refine, and implement the best solution.

>The model is called the Double Diamond because it alternates between exploring many possibilities (divergent thinking) and narrowing down options (convergent thinking).
