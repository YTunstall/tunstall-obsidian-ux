**Subject**: UX in healthcare

**Summary**:

>The article presents the design of a sensor-based decision support system to help nursing staff in elderly care. The goal was to improve efficiency and care quality by collecting health data from sensors and presenting it in an easy-to-understand way for caregivers. 

Conclusions:

- Manually analyzing large amounts of health data is is too time-consuming 
- Healthcare workers are willing to use AI but prefer to make their own final decisions rather than to rely on AI completely
- Healthcare staff report that limited time and high staff turnover create a need for decision support systems that summarize health data and help identify important changes
