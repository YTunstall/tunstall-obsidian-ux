Fidelity refers to the level of detail and realism in a design prototype. As fidelity increases, the prototype more closely resembles the final product in terms of appearance, content, and functionality.

>**Low fidelity**: A wireframe that is very basic and contains no graphics. It mostly consists of placeholders to give a sense of the layout. Not interactive.
>
>**Mid fidelity**: Contains more complete content and media, such as images and icons, with some placeholders. Uses Lorem ipsum text. Not interactive or only minimally interactive.
>
>**High fidelity**: Contains complete content with the correct media and text. Fully interactive.
